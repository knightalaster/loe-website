AERO SMITH JR.
Aero Smith Jr.
KING OF EMERALD KINGDOM

Overview

Son of Aero. A master swordsman who became king after recovering the Emerald capable of reviving the princess. His rise transformed him from explorer into ruler of an entire kingdom.

Weapon

Swordsmanship

Kingdom

Emerald Kingdom

Allies

MC, Ken, Wilden, Amber

Status

Alive

Important Events

Explored the island of kingdoms with his crew, defeated goblins within the mines, discovered the Emerald hidden within a dragon cave, revived the princess, and inherited the throne after marrying into the royal family.

Bitey Conflict

During Bitey’s manipulation of Emerald Kingdom, Aero Smith Jr. initially accepted cooperation to protect the kingdom from destruction caused by Evil Franxx. Over time, the kingdom advanced technologically while corruption spread throughout its society.
